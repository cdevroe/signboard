# Signboard 1.7.5

## What's New

- Fixed Windows Signboard links being interpreted as CLI commands. Links now reach the desktop protocol handler without a `--` separator or registry workaround.
- Fixed Kanban card dragging with label and date filters active. Filtered cards can move within and between lists while hidden cards retain their relative order.
- Prevented card-area drag gestures from moving whole lists. Date-filtered cards moved into completed lists immediately leave the filtered view while retaining their dates.
