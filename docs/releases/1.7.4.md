# Signboard 1.7.4

## What's New

- Added 42 color schemes, bringing the total to 51. The new schemes include contrast adjustments for text, metadata, links, and buttons in both Light and Dark modes.
- Planner now uses the selected board’s color scheme across Calendar, This Week, Day, and Agenda.
- Opening another board’s card in Planner preserves the board you were viewing in Kanban or Table, along with its search and filters.
- Choose Light or Dark by clicking its preview in Settings > Appearance, or choose Auto to follow your operating system. Your existing mode is preserved when upgrading. The Board-menu switch has moved into Appearance; the keyboard shortcut still toggles modes.

- Prevented unchanged editor callbacks from overwriting newer external card edits.

Thanks to [Marcus Holtz (@MarcusHoltz)](https://github.com/MarcusHoltz) for the color schemes in [#58](https://github.com/cdevroe/signboard/issues/58) and Planner theme inheritance in [#59](https://github.com/cdevroe/signboard/issues/59).
